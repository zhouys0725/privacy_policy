Getting started

Sign in

The app supports Apple, Google and enabled email sign-in. Use an account you will keep, and sign in before linking another identity to avoid creating an empty book.

Create a book

Choose a currency and enter account balances, income and actual expenses. Bills and subscription plans are tracked separately and are not treated as money already spent.

Debts and goals

Debt repayment and risk modules appear only after you enter a debt. Users without debt do not see those modules.

Assistant

Ask how much you owe, what remains this month or how far a savings goal is. Every book change is previewed before you confirm it.

Account, export and deletion

In the app you can export a confirmed book, revoke active sessions, and delete a book with its related drafts, conversations and change records. Deleting a book does not close your entire account or cancel an Apple, Stripe or merchant subscription.

To close your account, handle former Vois data or delete other personal information, email zhouys0725@gmail.com from your registered address. We verify ownership before processing and explain any records that must be retained for legal, tax or payment-audit purposes.

Former Vois users

Yuno is the new direction for the former “Vois” app. Old voice-diary entries are not automatically merged into financial books. If you have an active membership, do not purchase it again.

For an export, membership review or account question, contact us from the registered email with “Vois migration” in the subject. We will verify the former account and provide the applicable next step.

Membership and payments

Charges occur only when the app actually presents a purchase option. Review the price, currency, term, auto-renewal and cancellation method before paying. iOS purchases are handled by Apple and managed under system Subscriptions; other channels follow the terms shown at checkout.

Restoring a purchase does not merge books across different sign-in identities. If payment succeeded but your membership is missing, keep the store order ID and contact us rather than purchasing again.

AI and data use

AI analysis is opt-in. When enabled, your question, necessary recent context and the limited book excerpts and computed results selected by the server to answer it are sent to the configured model service. The entire book is not sent at once. With AI off, rule-based answers, records, planning and export remain available.

AI does not make transfers, repayments or securities trades. Every book change requires you to review and confirm it.

Account security

Never share your password, verification code or sign-in token. If you notice an unexpected session, revoke it in the app and contact us. This public support page will never ask for a full card number or Apple account password.

Frequently asked

Why do I not see debt features?

Debt features appear only after you enter a debt, so users without one do not see unnecessary modules.

Why does a projection differ from my lender statement?

Projections use the balances, dates, rates and fee assumptions you enter. Contractual interest, penalties or payment order may differ; rely on the lender statement.

Does deleting a subscription plan cancel the merchant renewal?

No. The plan is a record for your budget. Cancel the actual renewal with the merchant or payment provider.

Will my book appear on a new phone?

It remains available under the same sign-in identity after server sync. Clearing local sign-in does not delete the server book; deleting the book does.
