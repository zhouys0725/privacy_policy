Photo Director support

Help with your camera, account or subscription.

Contact the GravityArc team

zhouyushi@gravityarc.top

Include your app version, iOS version and what happened. Never send passwords, verification codes or card details. Share a photo only if needed and you have permission.

Camera or saving is unavailable

Check camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. Camera capture, framing and color tools run on your iPhone. If the cloud service is temporarily unavailable, local tools remain available for an already signed-in user.

Purchases and refunds

Use Restore Purchases in the app if a subscription is missing. Apple handles billing and refund decisions. Removing the app or deleting an account does not cancel a subscription.

  • Manage or cancel an Apple subscription
  • Request a refund from Apple

Account and data

In Account & Cloud, you can sign out, delete saved cloud advice or permanently delete your account. Account deletion removes account information, cloud advice/request records and login sessions; necessary subscription bindings and spent-allowance records remain. It does not remove your Photos library or cancel an Apple subscription. If you cannot sign in, email us from your account address for help verifying ownership.

Cloud advice availability

Public cloud AI analysis is not currently available. Available sign-in and subscription options are shown in the app. You will not be asked to upload a photo automatically.
