Privacy policy

Photo Director (also called DirectorCam or Director) is a GravityArc iPhone camera app. Camera guidance and photo editing run on your device. Cloud features use only the photos you explicitly choose to send.

Effective and last updated: November 13, 2026

Who to contact

GravityArc operates Photo Director. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact zhouyushi@gravityarc.top. This policy covers Photo Director and its account/cloud service, not other GravityArc products.

Camera and photo permissions

The app uses the camera for capture and on-device framing, pose and color guidance. It uses device motion to help you level the camera. Live camera frames and pose observations are not automatically uploaded. We do not create a face-recognition identity or use these observations to identify you.

Saving a photo requests permission to add it to your Photos library. Choosing a photo for cloud analysis uses the system photo picker; it does not grant the app unrestricted access to your library. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. The app does not request your location. Your original photos remain under your control in Photos.

Accounts and sign-in

The account service stores your email address, account identifier and, for password sign-in, a password hash. If you choose an available Google or Apple sign-in option, the provider processes the sign-in and supplies identity information such as an account identifier and email address. We use it to authenticate your account, not to access your social posts, contacts or advertising accounts.

Session credentials and pending cloud-request identifiers are stored in this app’s device-only Keychain. The service stores session records and hashed sign-in/rate-limit identifiers to protect accounts. Network infrastructure processes connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure requests.

Optional cloud photo advice

When available, cloud analysis starts only after you select a photo and tap the upload/analyze button. The app prepares a reduced-size copy without location or capture metadata and sends that copy and your chosen advice language to the service and its AI provider. Our account database stores the request identifier, a fingerprint of the input, status, generated advice and usage records; it does not store the uploaded image itself.

Cloud AI analysis is not currently offered in the public service. Before enabling it, we will identify the AI provider and its applicable data handling here. Do not send sensitive, confidential or other people’s photos without appropriate permission. AI advice may be inaccurate; it is not a biometric assessment.

Subscriptions and service providers

Apple handles in-app purchases, renewals, payment details and refund requests. We use verified subscription information, purchase identifiers, account bindings and usage counters to grant access and prevent duplicate use; we do not receive your full card details.

Cloudflare hosts the account service and its database. Google and Apple process sign-in when you choose their available options. These providers may process data outside your country. Their services are also subject to their own privacy policies: Cloudflare, Google and Apple.

The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking features. We do not sell your personal information or use your photos for targeted advertising.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Local camera settings and capture allowance remain on your device. Photos saved to your library remain there until you delete them using Photos.
  • Account information and cloud advice remain with your account until you delete them. In Account & Cloud, delete individual advice or permanently delete the account. Account deletion removes account details, cloud advice/request records and all login sessions.
  • Necessary pseudonymous subscription bindings, spent-allowance counters and abuse-prevention records can remain after account deletion. They prevent transferring an existing subscription to a new account or resetting spent allowance. They are not an active account or a saved photo. Contact us about retained records; deletion may be limited where needed to prevent fraud or meet applicable obligations.
  • Signing out clears the device’s current login state; it does not delete your cloud account. Deleting advice, signing out, deleting an account or uninstalling does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it through Apple.

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of personal information by email. We may need to verify account ownership; do not email passwords, verification codes or card details. Rights and applicable exceptions depend on your location. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
