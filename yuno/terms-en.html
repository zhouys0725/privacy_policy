Service scope

Yuno records confirmed income, expenses, account balances, debt terms and savings goals, then uses those records to provide planning, repayment scenarios and financial answers. Yuno does not make bank transfers, repay loans, trade securities, buy insurance or cancel a merchant subscription for you.

Accounts and review

Protect your sign-in credentials. Different sign-in identities are not merged automatically. Review the preview and confirm changes before they are saved.

Projections and professional advice

Projections depend on the balances, dates, income and debt terms you enter. Future income can be delayed and contractual interest or fees control your statement. Debt-risk notices do not predict a specific lawsuit date and are not legal, investment or tax advice.

AI and subscriptions

AI helps interpret intent and extract fields you explicitly provide. Amounts and projections use book rules; you must enable AI analysis. Subscribe only when the app presents a purchase option, and review the price, currency, term and cancellation method before paying.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service for fraud, infringement, API abuse or disruption. You can export a confirmed book and its change records or delete a book. Removing a plan does not cancel a loan obligation or a merchant charge.
