Scope and operator

This policy applies to the Yuno mobile app, mini program and related online services. The operator is Beijing Gravity Arc Technology Center (“we”, “us” or “our”).

Yuno records income, expenses, bills, debts, repayments and savings goals that you enter yourself, then uses those records to provide planning and answers. Yuno does not connect to your bank and does not automatically read bank statements, brokerage accounts or insurance policies.

Information we collect

Account and sign-in information

  • Email address, Apple or Google account identifier and the email returned by that provider (Apple may return a private relay address). If WeChat sign-in is enabled, we process the mini program AppID, OpenID and any phone number you choose to authorize.
  • Sign-in challenges, session identifiers, token versions, account status and security records.

Financial information you enter

  • Book name and currency, account balances, actual income and expenses, recurring bills, subscription plans, debt principal and interest, repayment dates, savings goals, notes, imported files and earmarked funds.
  • Rule calculations, repayment scenarios, planning progress, confirmed change history and pending edits generated from those records.

Financial assistant and conversations

We call a configured AI service only when you enable AI analysis and submit a question. The request includes your question, selected recent conversation turns or a summary for context, and the limited ledger excerpts and computed results that the server selects to answer that question. We do not send the entire book at once. Questions, answers, tool status and necessary summaries are stored with the book.

Payments and membership

When you purchase through an enabled Apple, Stripe or WeChat payment channel, that provider processes your payment credentials. We receive the product identifier, transaction or subscription status, amount paid and timing needed to verify entitlements. We do not store full card numbers.

Operational and security logs

Our infrastructure may process request time, API route, error code, device type, network and security events to keep the service available and prevent abuse. The current app does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you, maintain your account and sync the book you select.
  • Record and reconcile amounts, produce bill and repayment plans, calculate savings progress and answer your financial questions.
  • Save changes after you confirm them and prevent duplicate or unauthorized writes.
  • Verify membership, restore purchases and support billing or refund issues.
  • Diagnose failures, protect accounts and systems, and enforce rate limits and abuse controls.
  • Meet applicable legal, tax, accounting or dispute-handling obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use your financial records for advertising targeting.

Sharing, service providers and international processing

We share only what is necessary with service providers in these categories, and limit their use to the service they provide:

  • Cloudflare for API hosting, operational logs and D1 storage; Neon or a compatible database host when a PostgreSQL environment is enabled.
  • Apple, Google and WeChat to verify identity when you choose that sign-in method, and to process purchases through the payment channel you select.
  • Configured model services such as Alibaba Cloud Bailian / DashScope only when you enable AI analysis and submit a question. A configured embedding model may help retrieve similar past conversations.
  • Email or SMS providers only when a verification-code sign-in method is enabled and you request a code.
  • Stripe or another payment provider only when the corresponding purchase option is enabled and you choose to pay.

These providers may process data in other countries or regions. We use contractual, access-control and other appropriate safeguards, and require providers to process personal information only on our instructions.

Retention and deletion

Account information and your current book remain while you use the service. To avoid accidental overwrites, the server keeps the current book, necessary change records, conversations and unexpired drafts; it does not keep full historical snapshots of every book version.

You can export a book in the app, or delete a book together with its related drafts, conversations and change records. Payment and subscription records may be retained for the period required for financial, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes. Operational and security logs are kept for a limited operational period.

Automatic sign-in credentials are stored in protected device storage for the native app. They are no longer used after you sign out or revoke the session.

Your rights and choices

You can view, correct and export confirmed books, revoke active sessions, disable AI analysis, delete a book, and contact us to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing already carried out on that basis.

Deleting a book is not the same as closing your entire account, and it does not cancel a subscription held by Apple, Stripe or a merchant. To close your account or handle a third-party authorization, contact us through Support. Where law requires us to retain a record, we will explain the reason and any available scope.

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, account-status checks, session revocation, data minimization and server-side authorization. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Protect your sign-in credentials and avoid saving a session on a device you do not trust.

AI and financial projection limits

AI helps interpret questions, extract fields you explicitly provide and select data tools. Amounts, repayment progress and projections are calculated by book rules. Before a book change is saved, Yuno shows a preview and asks you to confirm it. Enabling AI means the relevant question and necessary context will be sent to the configured model service; with AI disabled, rule-based answers are used.

Projections depend on your inputs and assumptions. They are not a lender payoff quote and do not guarantee investment returns, approvals or legal outcomes. Debt-risk notices do not predict a specific lawsuit date and are not legal, investment, tax or other professional advice.

Children and minors

The service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. Material changes will be indicated in the app or on this page with a revised date. Please review the updated policy before continuing to use the service.

Contact us

For privacy, account deletion, data export or related requests, contact support@gravity.cn.
